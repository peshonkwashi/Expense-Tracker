## Scoring guide *(for the researcher — do not give to the participant)*

**SUS score (Section B), per participant:**
1. Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): subtract 1 from the response → `(x − 1)`.
2. Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): subtract the response from 5 → `(5 − x)`.
3. Add the ten adjusted values (range 0–40), then **multiply by 2.5** → a score out of **100**.
4. Report the **mean SUS across participants**. Interpretation (Bangor et al.): `< 51` poor, `51–68` OK, `68` average, `68–80.3` good, `> 80.3` excellent.

**Section C / D Likert:** report the mean (and spread) per item across participants;
highlight the highest- and lowest-rated features for the Chapter 8 discussion, and
use the "worked as expected?" column to report a per-feature success rate.

**Sections E / F:** group the free-text responses into themes (e.g. *clarity*,
*trust/privacy*, *missing features*, *mobile*). The recurring themes are the
qualitative findings for Chapter 8; the adoption answers (F1, F6) and the
suggested features/barriers (F3, F5) are the basis of the Chapter 9
recommendations and future-work section.
