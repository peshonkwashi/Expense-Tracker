Salary-Linked Expense Tracker & Budget Advisor — User Acceptance Test

Thank you for testing this application. You are evaluating a privacy-first personal finance tool that links spending analysis and budget advice to your payday cycle. All data stays strictly on the local device.

Part 1 — Guided Testing Tasks (15–20 Minutes)

Please complete the five short tasks below. During Task 1, please try to complete the steps without asking for hints so we can test how intuitive the first-time setup is.

Task 1: First-Time Profile Setup & Bank Statement Upload (Target: Under 5 Minutes)

  1. Open the application link provided by the facilitator. On the Set up your profile screen, enter:
    • Your name: Your first name (or Tester P01)
    • Monthly salary (ZMW): 12000  |  Day of the month you are paid: 25
    • Password: Choose any password of at least 8 characters and confirm it.
  2. Read the data privacy notice, tick "I understand how my data is stored", and click Create profile.
  3. Go to Transactions, locate Manual CSV Upload, select the sample bank statement file (bank_statement_P01.csv), and click Upload and categorise.
  4. Click Dashboard in the top menu to view your financial overview. (Tell the facilitator when you reach the Dashboard.)

Task 2: Checking Automatic Categories, Correcting a Transaction & Receipt Items

  1. Open Transactions and check the Categorisation model accuracy badge at the top.
  2. Browse the list of imported transactions. Notice how merchants (e.g., Shoprite, ZESCO, Yango, Netflix) were automatically sorted into Essential and Discretionary categories.
  3. Find any transaction row, change its category using the dropdown under Correct it, and click Save. Observe the confirmation message showing that the system learned from your correction.
  4. On any debit row, click or add items manually (or upload a receipt photo via Scan Receipt) and add a sample receipt item (e.g., Bread, Qty 1, Amount 25).

Task 3: Salary-Cycle Insights, Subscriptions & Budget Advice

  1. Open Dashboard and check:
    • Your Salary-Cycle Spending pattern (how much is spent right after payday on the 25th vs later in the month).
    • The Alerts / Nudges section (plain-language warnings when a category crosses 80% of its recommended budget).
    • The Recurring Subscriptions summary showing monthly charges (Netflix, DSTV, Spotify) and their yearly cost.
  2. Open Budget and click Why this number? under any category to read how its recommended limit was calculated from your salary.

Task 4: Savings Goals, Monthly Reports & Data Backup

  1. Open Savings Goals, add a new goal (e.g., Emergency Fund, Target: 6000 ZMW, Date: 6 months ahead), and click Add goal. Check whether the monthly required saving is marked On track or Shortfall, then log a 500 ZMW contribution.
  2. Open Reports and switch between months to see your total income, total spending, net savings rate, and category breakdown.
  3. Open Settings to view the model accuracy badges and test Export database backup.

Task 5: Mobile Phone / App Mode Check

  1. View the app on a mobile phone (or mobile screen view) and tap Install App / Add to Home Screen if prompted.
  2. Open Transactions on the mobile screen and confirm that the table cleanly stacks into readable cards and that buttons are easy to tap.
