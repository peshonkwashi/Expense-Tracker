Add a cash transaction

For money that won't appear on a bank statement: a minibus fare, market produce, cash you were paid. Card and mobile-money payments arrive with your statement, so adding them here as well would count them twice.

Cash you take out at an ATM or a mobile-money agent is filed as Cash Withdrawal and not counted as spending, so log what you spend the cash on here.

Leave it to the app, or pick one; the categoriser learns from the ones you pick.
