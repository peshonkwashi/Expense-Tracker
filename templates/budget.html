How this budget was built

  1. Essentials first. Each essential category is funded at its forecast plus a 5% buffer ({{ budget.essential_total|money }} {{ currency }} in total).
  2. Savings commitments next. Your goals require {{ budget.goal_total|money }} {{ currency }} a month to land on time.
  3. Discretionary spending last. What remains ({{ budget.discretionary_total|money }} {{ currency }}) is shared across your wants.
  4. Anything left over is savings. That comes to {{ budget.recommended_savings|money }} {{ currency }} this month.

This follows the needs-based allocation hierarchy rather than a fixed 50/30/20 split, so it adapts to what your life actually costs.
